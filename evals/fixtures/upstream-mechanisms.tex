\section{Local matrix convention}
In this note, $\R=M_2(\mathbb R)$ is the algebra of real two by two matrices.
Every matrix called positive definite below is also assumed symmetric.
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:positive-spectrum}
If $A\in\R$ is positive definite, every eigenvalue of $A$ is positive.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For an eigenpair with nonzero real vector $v$, positivity gives
$0<v^\top Av=\lambda v^\top v$, hence $\lambda>0$.
The real symmetric spectral theorem supplies such eigenvectors.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:inverse}
Every positive definite $A\in\R$ is invertible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-spectrum}, no eigenvalue is zero.
The real symmetric spectral theorem therefore gives an invertible diagonalization.
\end{proof}
